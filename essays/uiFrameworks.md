How many people are actually experts on a subject? For that matter what are the criteria of being an expert? These are questions that came to mind as I started incorporating Bootstrap into my html design process and scoured various forums and Stack Overflow for help. Learning Bootstrap is almosr like learning a whole new language except it is intereocen with another language and it becomes a pidgin mishmash.

Perhaps if one was an expert in at least one of the languages the result wouldn't be so bad but such is not my situation. Bootstrap has a lot of similarities with html and is meant to be an aid but I am definitely feeling the need to go back to basics and learn html! One day I will be able to make a beautiful html quilt in tye frame of Bootstrap.
